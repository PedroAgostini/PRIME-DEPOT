Do you deliver to my job site? How far do you go?

Yes. We deliver across Massachusetts, straight to your job site or lumber yard.

Do I need to buy a full pallet to get this price?

No. There are no minimums. Buy exactly what your current job needs at direct importer pricing.

How do I place an order?

Send us your cut list by phone or through the quote form below. We confirm stock and delivery right away, no long forms.

Are the prices on the site real? Do you discount big orders?

Yes, the prices you see are the prices you pay. For large projects, talk to our sales team about volume pricing.

Can I paint it, or does it only come in white?

It ships white and never needs paint, which saves time and money. If the project calls for color, it is fully paintable in any color.

Is it easy to cut? Do I need special tools?

No special tools. Our premium rigid PVC cuts and works much like traditional wood, without wood's defects.

Will it hold up to Massachusetts moisture and snow?

Yes, that is the main advantage. It is 100% waterproof, will not swell or rot, is termite proof and UV-stabilized, with a 30 to 50 year service life.
